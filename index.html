Paste your playlist

Works everywhere. Open your playlist link in a new Safari tab, copy all the text it shows (it starts with #EXTM3U), and paste it here.

Import a playlist file

Pick an .m3u or .m3u8 file saved on your iPhone (Files app, iCloud, downloads). Nothing is uploaded; it is read on this device.

Load from M3U URL

Needs the Helper below unless your provider allows browsers to connect directly. Use an http:// link exactly as your provider gave it.

Xtream Codes login

Server, username and password from your provider. Needs the Helper below unless your provider allows browsers to connect directly.

Portal login (Stalker / MAC) needs Helper

For providers that give you a portal address and a MAC address instead of a username. This works only with the Helper turned on, and it is the least certain of the options: portals differ a lot.

Helper off

Optional. Many providers refuse connections that come straight from a web page. The helper is a small free service you run yourself (setup steps are in the guide) that fetches streams for the page the way a normal player app would.

Add to Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) to use this like an app, full-screen with its own icon.
